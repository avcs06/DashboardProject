## S04 Your dashboards (/dashboards)
Pick up the dashboard the member edited last, or start a new one, after signing in or returning to the app.
Roles: member · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F05, F07

1. Reads "Your dashboards" and that their dashboards are in the list that stays with them as they work.
2. Can open the dashboard they edited last, which goes to /dashboards/[dashboardId].
3. Sees how many dashboards they have in total.

When the dashboard the member edited last was deleted in the meantime:
   1. Reads that the dashboard they edited last is gone.
   2. Can choose a dashboard from the dashboard list in the frame, which goes to /dashboards/[dashboardId].

When the member has no dashboards yet:
   1. Reads that there is no dashboard edited yet because they have no dashboards yet.
   2. Can choose "Create" in the frame, which creates a dashboard named 'Untitled dashboard' and goes to /dashboards/[dashboardId].

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/dashboards`.
Loads its records through: listDashboards, getDashboard.
