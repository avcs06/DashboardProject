## S01 Your day, all in one dashboard (/)
Tell a visitor that the app builds private personal dashboards of notes, counters, checklists and links, and start them toward signing up.
Roles: member · Frame: FR01 · Flows: F01, F02

1. Reads the headline "Your day, all in one dashboard".
2. Reads the line under it: "Build a personal dashboard of notes, counters, checklists and links, and keep the small things you track every day in one private place."
3. Can choose "Create your first dashboard", the main action, which goes to /sign-up.
4. Sees a preview of a dashboard called 'Morning routine' with a note called 'Launch plan' holding the text 'Call the dentist at noon', a counter called 'Glasses of water' showing 3, a checklist with 'Review the draft' checked and 'Buy oat milk' unchecked, and a link list with a link called 'MDN Web Docs', and the preview changes no records.
5. Reads "Notes that stay put" and "Keep plain text notes on the same dashboard as everything else you track."
6. Reads "Counters you can nudge" and "Track a number with a label and move it up or down by one whenever it changes."
7. Reads "Checklists you can tick off" and "Add items, tick them off and reword them as your day changes."
8. Reads "Links worth keeping" and "Save useful pages by title and web address, then open them from your dashboard."
